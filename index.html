Install on your Home Screen

iPhone and iPad require you to add this app from Safari:

  1. Open this page in Safari and tap the Share button (square with an upward arrow).
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Turn on Open as Web App if available, then tap Add.

If Add to Home Screen is missing, tap Edit Actions at the bottom of the Share menu and add it.
